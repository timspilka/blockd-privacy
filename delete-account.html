Delete your account

Analog Gallery — com.analog.gallery

To request deletion of your account and all data stored with it, send an email from the address your account is registered with, to:

REPLACE-WITH-YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL

Put Delete my account in the subject. No other details are needed — sending from the registered address is what identifies you.

We confirm within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Everything held on our side for your account:

  • Your account itself and the email address attached to it
  • Every image file backed up to the cloud
  • All details about your archive — rolls, film stocks, cameras, frame numbers, tags, favourites and trash dates
  • Your subscription entitlement record

This cannot be undone. Once the deletion runs, we have no copy of any of it.

What stays, and why

  • The copy on your phone. Deleting your account does not touch the archive stored locally in the app. Uninstall Analog Gallery to remove that, or delete rolls in the app first.
  • Payment records. Records of purchases are kept for 10 years, as Swiss accounting law requires (Art. 958f CO). These contain the transaction, not your archive.
  • Google Play's own records. Your purchase history sits with Google Play and is governed by Google's privacy policy, not ours.

Cancel your subscription first. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — billing runs through Google Play, and we cannot stop it on your behalf. Cancel it in the Play Store under Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions, otherwise you may keep being charged for an account that no longer exists.

If you only want to delete some of it

You do not need to delete your account to remove individual photos. In the app, deleting a photo or a roll moves it to the trash, where it stays for 30 days before being permanently removed from both your phone and the cloud copy. Emptying the trash removes it immediately.

If you want the cloud copy gone but your account kept — for example after cancelling a subscription — say so in your email and we will do only that.
